# Sample media

Stand-ins for a school's own photographs, used by the fixture content and therefore by
every published demonstration. A real institution supplies its own; nothing here ships
in a school's site except by its own configuration.

- `crest.svg` — a drawn mark, not a photograph. A school replaces it with its logo.
- `*.jpg` — photographs from [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com), used under the
  [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license), which permits commercial use
  without attribution. They are resized and re-encoded here for the web.

The people shown are not the people named beside them. The demonstrations carry sample
data throughout and say so.
